Wacom Bridge

Work remotely as if you were in the office

Wacom Bridge connects your local Wacom device to your remote work environment —cloud, VDI, server— with full driver compatibility. Pressure, tilt, ExpressKeys and application-specific settings, all working remotely.

Wacom Bridge for creative professionals

Creative studios working with virtualized desktops (Citrix, AWS WorkSpaces) or in hybrid setups need their Cintiq Pro to work the same remotely as locally. Wacom Bridge is the solution.

✓
Full pressure and tilt in remote sessions No loss of functionality. The Pro Pen 3's 8,192 pressure levels work identically on the remote server as locally.
✓
No USB redirection Controller-to-controller connection —without the latency and compatibility issues of USB-over-IP solutions.
✓
Per-application settings preserved ExpressKeys shortcuts, sensitivities and application profiles (Photoshop, After Effects, Blender) are maintained in the remote environment.
✓
Remote demo via Teams, Zoom or Webex Show your work to clients and collaborators by sharing the remote session directly from meeting software.
✓
Windows Ink y Wintab Compatible with both protocols, locally and remotely. No software limitations.
